# Banyan Skyway props

`forest-props-key.png` is the original 1536 × 1024 output from built-in ImageGen, saved on 13 September 2026. It is 2,142,086 bytes. It supplies the branch, portal and checkpoint lantern in `components/game/PartyMinigame.tsx`; the existing forest panel in `game-worlds.png` remains the backdrop. These are original tropical fantasy assets, not extracted MapleStory artwork.

The first generation did not produce usable transparency. A second ImageGen call replaced the background with a magenta matte. `forest-art.ts` resolves that matte into an offscreen transparent canvas once on load, including magenta edge spill suppression. The original PNG stays intact. No per-frame pixel processing is used. Measured sprite frames and the branch's moss landing plane are recorded in that renderer. Branch caps and depth keep their size as the centre stretches for the wide starting platform; portal and lantern retain their source aspect ratios.

Delivery uses `/api/art?file=party/forest-props-key.png`. The existing binding-aware route accepts the new file and falls back to the bundled asset. An actual remote R2 object/binding is still unverified.

EXACTLY THREE isolated sprites with large clear transparent padding and no overlap. Upper half: one long horizontal moss-covered banyan branch platform, spans x=100 to1436 and y=100 to400, level walkable moss surface at y=150, braided woody roots taper beneath, ferns only at extreme ends, slightly irregular natural silhouette but flat level top across middle90%; no landscape rectangle or background. Lower left quadrant x100..650,y520..970: one free-standing upright oval portal in a twisted banyan root arch, roughly450x450, glowing luminous turquoise interior with subtle swirling star dust, natural bark rim and moss base, complete arch and base visible, no text. Lower right quadrant x950..1300,y600..900: one small hanging brass lantern checkpoint beacon on a short wooden post with moss foot, warm amber glow, entire object visible. Crisp fully isolated silhouette alpha, no floor plane, no cast background shadow, no labels, no UI, no icons or extra objects. Consistent frontal side-view platformer camera. Assets must remain readable at platform widths100..250 pixels and portal70pixels. Do not create a concept scene; create only the three separated deployable assets.
