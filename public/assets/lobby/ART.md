# Singapore lobby artwork

Original images generated with the built-in Image Gen tool for the party-lobby brief. The user's Fall Guys lobby montage supplies composition/mood, while the existing Our Block pixel roster remains the character artwork.

Each backdrop is 1672 × 941, encoded as WebP at quality 88 (about 2.1 MB total for all six). The transparent podium is encoded at 720 × 360. Source PNGs remain in the local generation archive; these WebPs are self-contained runtime assets. No remote image dependency, additional model or third-party asset package is required.

Shared brief: wide Singapore environment, clear centre/foreground for the character ring; colourful polished 3D illustration, detailed buildings along the sides/back, warm lighting. No foreground characters, UI or podium baked into the backgrounds. These are interpretive, era-inspired lobby scenes, not measured historical reconstructions or replacements for the researched Blender worlds.

- `1950.webp`: Art Deco Cathay-inspired cinema, five-foot-way shophouses, trishaw, roadside kopi/satay and period cars. Sunset amber and teal. The cinema/street/harbour arrangement is a fictional composite.
- `1965.webp`: old Singapore River shophouses, tongkangs/bumboats, kampong edge, palms and sunshine. No modern skyline or Merlion.
- `1975.webp`: Worlds amusement-park composite, cinema/dance-hall fronts, carnival stalls and distant Ferris wheel, warm bulbs under a blue-purple night sky.
- `1987.webp`: HDB corridors/void decks, stylised mosaic dragon playground, provision shop and terrazzo chess table. Not a measured reconstruction of the Toa Payoh playground.
- `2005.webp`: mature HDB estate, elevated red-and-white MRT, sheltered seating and arcade storefront, warm sundown. No MBS or Supertrees.
- `2026.webp`: rounded toy-like Marina Bay waterfront, Merlion, Marina Bay Sands silhouette and Supertrees, coral/cyan/purple evening lights.
- `podium.webp`: isolated glossy magenta cylindrical platform, thin cream rim, teal base trim, oval top seen slightly from above; true alpha transparency.

The six scenes vary in realism and lighting; the constant pixel roster, pink podiums and navy/yellow controls tie the eras together. Lobby years and labels come from `EXPLORATION_ERAS`, keeping the automatically rotating scenes aligned with the actual exploration choices.
