# Our Block — submission copy

## Description

Explore Singapore's past with your friends. Roam 2.5D neighbourhoods, then compete in flag erasers, teh tarik, hawker orders, climbing and getai rhythm games. One shared screen brings the block together; your phones become the controllers.

## How did you use Astra?

We used Astra with GPT Image 2.5 to turn everyday Singapore into a world people can explore and play together. Astra helped shape the experience; GPT Image 2.5 supplied the characters, era-inspired artwork and minigame imagery that give Our Block its identity.

The image work is part of the play. Eight local mascots—including a kopi-cup robot, kueh and a mosaic dragon—carry across the experience alongside four fictional neighbours in three life stages. Teh Tarik uses separately generated stall and pourer layers so the tea and cups can respond live. Flag Erasers uses a classroom matched to the existing character artwork. Last Order turns generated kopi, toast, kueh and laksa into recognisable order choices.

We refined the images through references and targeted edits: adjusting the pouring pose, maintaining character style and framing artwork around moving game objects. Generated world references helped guide Blender scenes, while historical research informed the neighbourhood details. The worlds are stylised interpretations of Singapore life.

Astra also supported Product Design iterations, Blender scene work, ambient activity, QR phone joining, minigame controls, scoring and asset delivery. We steered it through feedback and playtesting. GPT Image 2.5 gave the block its visual character; Astra helped make those images playable.

## Play note

Open the game on the central computer, select Download all assets and wait for completion. Use a reliable connection or mobile hotspot if venue Wi-Fi is slow. Keep all devices connected during multiplayer.
